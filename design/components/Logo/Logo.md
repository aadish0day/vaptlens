# Logo

The VAPTLens lockup: the lens-reticle mark in `lens` plus the wordmark "VAPT" in `ink` and "Lens" in `lens`.

**Provide:** `size` (mark height in px, default 24; the word sets at 0.75× that), `wordmark={false}` for the mark alone (nav collapsed, favicon-sized slots).

- Use it at the left of the top nav at `size={22}` and on the report cover at 40–56.
- The mark inherits `currentColor` via `.vl-logo-mark` (set to `lens`); don't recolor it with severity or chart colors.
- Minimum clear space around the lockup: the mark's own radius (half its size).
- For `<img>` slots (favicons, exports) use the uploaded `vaptlens-mark-dark.svg` on dark grounds, `vaptlens-mark-light.svg` on light, `vaptlens-app-icon.svg` for app tiles.
- Placeholder mark: replace once a final logo exists.
