# ReportHeader

Executive Report cover band and title block, in the three report themes.

**Provide:** `theme` (`slate` | `navy` | `crimson`), `title`, optional `kind` (band label), `meta` (scope · date · classification line).

- Reports always print on white paper: the band uses `report-*` with `on-report` text, titles are `ink` (light), and the underline is `report-rule`.
- The severity ramp inside the report uses the light theme values, whatever the app theme.
